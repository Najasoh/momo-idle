## Professions

- **Mining** — rocks and ore. Nodes have individual respawn times. Gem drop table 1/256.
- **Smithing** — smelt bars at a furnace. Varrock limited to bronze, iron, steel.
- **Anvil** — hammer bars into gear. Varrock limited to bronze, iron, steel.
- **Cooking** — cook raw food. Burn chance. Cook-o-matic 100 passive from Cook's Assistant.
- **Fishing** — spots with tools. Catch time varies.
- **Crafting** — spinning wheel (ball of wool, bow string).
- **Fletching** — knife in inventory opens fletching. Arrow shafts, headless arrows, shortbow.
- **Woodcutting** — trees need an axe. Tree (1), Oak (15), Willow (30), Yew (60).
- **Firemaking** — light logs with a tinderbox. Fail chance drops with level. Campfire enables cooking.
- **Farming** — hops patch. Flax seeds.
- **Thieving** — pickpocket (1.2s) and stalls. Resist stuns and damages. Man/Woman/Citizen, Varrock Guard, Tea stall, Silk stall.
- **Agility** — Varrock rooftop course (level 30, 70s). Mark of grace.
- **Prayer** — bury bones, sacrifice fish/ensouled at altar, recharge prayer points.
- **Sailing** — unlocked by Pandemonium quest.
- **Slayer** — xp from combat kills. Quests give slayer xp.

## Quests

- Cook's Assistant — egg, pot of flour, bucket of milk. 300 cooking xp, Cook-o-matic 100.
- Sheep Shearer — shear 20 sheep, spin 20 wool. 150 crafting xp.
- Doric's Quest — 6 clay, 4 copper, 2 iron. 1300 mining xp.
- The Ides of Milk — defeat Brutus. Mooleta, bottomless milk bucket.
- Fisher's Friend — 20 raw shrimp. 100 fishing xp.
- Woodchucker's Wood — 20 logs. 100 woodcutting xp.
- Cave Explorer — explore Talsy's cave. 1 quest point.
- Goblin Slayer — kill 100 goblins. 300 slayer xp.
- Imp Catcher — black/yellow/red/white beads. 875 magic xp, Amulet of accuracy.
- Witch's Potion — burnt meat, eye of newt, onion, rat's tail.
- Pirate's Treasure — Karamjan rum for Redbeard Frank. 2 quest points, one-eyed Hector's treasure.
- Rune Mysteries — talk to Aubury twice. 1 quest point.
- Pandemonium — sailing quest. Unlocks sailing skill.

## Tables

- **Gem table** — 1/256 chance. Uncut sapphire, emerald, ruby, diamond.
- **Rune table** — weighted runes (air most common, blood rarest). Outlaw, Black Knight.
- **Coin table** — 3/5/10/15/25/50 coins by weight. Outlaw, Black Knight.
